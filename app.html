ThrowFit

How far back does the projector go for the screen you want, or how big a screen fits your room?

About this

Throw ratio = throw distance ÷ image width, and it uses the image width, not the diagonal. Width comes from the diagonal and aspect ratio. Distance is measured from the lens, and projectors with a zoom lens list a range, like 1.4 to 2.2. Matches the BenQ example: 1.13 throw ratio, 100 inch 16:9 image (about 7.2 ft wide) needs about 8.2 ft. Check your model's spec sheet; lens shift and keystone limits are not modelled, and spec ratios are nominal. About
